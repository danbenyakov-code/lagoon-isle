Delete your Lagoon Isle account

Lagoon Isle is published by Lagoon Isle (developer contact: info.naimly@gmail.com). You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Lagoon Isle and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to info.naimly@gmail.com with the subject "Lagoon Isle account deletion" and include the email address or Google account you used to sign in, plus your in-game nickname. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, user ID, sign-in method)
  • Your cloud-saved game progress, nickname, ranking entries and friends list

What may be kept

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law and for refunds; we do not store payment card details.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

Progress saved only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.
