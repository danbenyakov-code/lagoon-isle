Privacy Policy: Lagoon Isle

Last updated: October 8, 2026. Operator: Lagoon Isle. Contact: info.naimly@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with email or Google, we store a user ID, email address and display name (if provided). You can play as a guest without an account.
  • Game progress: level, items, coins and gems are stored on your device and, for signed-in players, in the cloud (Google Firebase).
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive card details. Purchase data is processed by RevenueCat to validate purchases.
  • Nickname and rankings: your chosen nickname, character, level and weekly points are shown to other players in rankings and friend lists. Do not use your full real name as a nickname.
  • Notifications: if you allow it, the game sends local reminders (for example when energy is full). You can turn them off in your phone settings.
  • Ads: the game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one for a reward, which may collect an advertising ID and device information. Where required, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

How we use it

To save and sync your progress, deliver your purchases, show rewarded ads and keep the game stable. We do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (sign-in and cloud save), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (purchase validation), Google Play and Apple App Store (payments). Each has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all progress at any time in the game: Settings, then Delete account. You can also email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
