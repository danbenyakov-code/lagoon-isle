Lagoon Isle: Merge Adventure

FULL:
Merge, build and explore in Lagoon Isle, a relaxing merge adventure on sunny tropical islands!

Lili inherited her grandma's island, but storms left it in ruins. Merge items, fill orders for friendly islanders and rebuild beach huts, cafés and resorts across 5 magical islands. Every island you restore reveals a new chapter of the story and brings you closer to grandma's last secret.

🏝️ MERGE TO CREATE
• Drag 2 matching items together to merge them into something better
• 6 merge chains and over 60 hand-painted items: drinks, beach treasures, gardens, tools, travel gear and lanterns
• Tap generators to make new items and complete your Merge Collection

🔨 BUILD AND DECORATE
• Rebuild and upgrade every building in 3 stages
• Decorate your island and watch it come back to life
• Sail to Lagoon Isle, Coral Bay, Palm Grove, Sunset Cliffs and the Hidden Isle

📖 A STORY TO DISCOVER
• Meet Rosa the Baker, Captain Sam, Kai the Surfer and more island friends
• Fill their orders to earn coins, gems and rewards

🎁 DAILY FUN AND REWARDS
• Free daily gift that grows every day you come back
• Daily tasks, reward chests and a tough Daily Hard Quest
• Collect cards in the album and complete sets for big prizes
• Weekly rankings

🌴 WHY PLAYERS LOVE IT
• Relaxing merge puzzle gameplay at your own pace
• Hand-painted tropical art and cozy island vibes
• Cloud save, so your island follows you to a new phone
• Available in English and Hebrew
